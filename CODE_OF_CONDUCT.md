# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want t1-paste to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of
experience, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good faith.
- Give and accept technical feedback gracefully.
- Credit other people's work.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Trolling, intimidation, or deliberate disruption.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Abusing project infrastructure or other users.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues to **conduct@transistorone.com**. Maintainers will review
reports and may warn, remove content, or ban participants. Enforcement decisions
are made in good faith and kept as private as practical.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests,
discussions, chat, and events.
